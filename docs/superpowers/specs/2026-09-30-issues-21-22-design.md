Issues: bonzanni/casa-plugin-quarterly-accounting#21 (bug, severity:medium), #22 (bug,
severity:low). Plugin 0.5.0 → 0.6.0 (schema 6 → 7: one column). Follows
`2026-09-30-issues-17-18-19-design.md` (0.5.0), whose Part A this extends.

Status: draft, design round D1 pending.

## Part A — issue #21: the cron pass and "go and check now" never outgrow a turn

### What happens today (0.5.0)

0.5.0 made a package round keep the property

> **Every Ellen turn does a bounded amount of work, and ends at a delegation whose
> notification starts the next one.**

The cron pass and the operator's "go and check now" (one flow: skill "Ellen: the pass")
still break it on both counts:

1. The sweep and judge delegations are `mode="sync"`. A specialist that answers within
   ~60 s answers inside Ellen's turn, and the turn carries on.
2. The sweep continuation hands out `work` of up to `TRIAGE_LIMIT` = 50 items
   (`work.work_list(conn)`, portals included and skipped by Ellen), and Ellen searches
   all of them in one turn.

Ellen's turn has 20 SDK turns; ~48 items plus two delegations took 21 in the #17
incident. When the Gmail turn dies before the judge step or `end_pass`, the store is
resumable but nothing calls `continue_pass`: the cron pass waits for the next Monday or
a stale reclaim (3 h, and only if something begins a pass), and a package asked for
meanwhile queues behind it; the operator's check gives no answer until asked again.

### The change: the package round's mechanism, for the check

    sweep (async delegation)                       ── Ellen's turn ends
    → [ Gmail chunk (≤ 10 items) → judge (async) ] ── Ellen's turn ends at each judge
        … repeated while items remain and the last chunk brought the count down
    → end_pass

- **Async delegations.** The sweep and the judge of a check are `mode="async"`: Casa
  answers `pending` at once and always notifies; Ellen records the delegation
  (`record_step(action="delegated")`, shared rule 2, unchanged) and ends her turn. The
  notification's `continue_pass(delegation_id, delegation_status)` (rule 5, unchanged)
  closes the step if the specialist never finished it, and continues.
- **Chunks.** The sweep continuation hands out at most `GMAIL_CHUNK` (10) items of the
  check's work, portals left out before the chunk is cut (0.5.0 D1: portals ahead in pid
  order must not fill a chunk). After a chunk with anything in it Ellen always starts the
  judge step and delegates it — the delegation is what ends her turn. The self-mail
  search and the Telegram inbox sweep stay with the sweep continuation's chunk only.
- **The loop.** A judge continuation of a check hands out the next chunk
  (`next: "gmail-round"`) under exactly the package rule (`steps._another_chunk`): the
  judge step ended `finished` (not errored, expired or stopped), this pass's Gmail probe
  is ok, and `0 < left < handed`, where `left` is the check's work still unsearched
  now and `handed` the same count when the last chunk was handed out. Otherwise
  `next: "end-pass"`. The count is a non-negative integer that must fall with every
  chunk; it rises inside a pass only by an operator's reply reviving an item (finite).

### What "the check's work" is

Package rounds count a search for a request when it was made after the request was
opened (`searched_seq > created_seq`) and the payment's facts are still the ones
searched for (`facts_fp`). A check counts the same thing against the pass: when the
sweep continuation first hands out a chunk it allocates a store sequence value
`since_seq` (`db.next_seq`) and keeps it on the pass's sweep step row. The check's work
is then

    fresh triage items, not portal, not searched since since_seq (same facts)

(`work.searched_since(d, seq)`; `searched_for(d, req)` becomes
`searched_since(d, req["created_seq"])`). An item Ellen marks `incomplete` with no
queries spends nothing and stays in the work; an item the judge pairs leaves triage and
so leaves the work.

`since_seq` is allocated once per pass (a second hand-out — a reclaimed continuation, the
next chunk — keeps it), so the loop's `handed` and `left` are always measured against
the same origin. The sweep row's carry keeps `since_seq`, `handed` (updated at every
hand-out, as the package's snapshot row does) and, at the first hand-out only,
`work_total` and `not_fresh` (the check's size, for the report below).

### The report

Ellen used to compute `{checked, total, not_searched}` from the one work list she held
in one turn; with chunks no turn sees the whole list, so the server computes it. Every
judge continuation of a check that handed out work carries

    report = {total: work_total + not_fresh,
              not_searched: left + not_fresh,
              checked: max(0, total - not_searched)}

(`left` measured at the continuation), replacing the report Ellen passed at the judge's
start. The outcome rule reads `report.not_searched > 0` as "an item not searched"
(→ `interrupted`). A check that never started a judge (Gmail down, or empty work and
nothing else owed) ends from the sweep continuation as today, Ellen's own report.

### The judge step repeats inside a check

`steps.start(step="judge")` restarts a judge step whose judgment finished — on the same
`pass_steps` row, clearing `pass_marker.claimed_step` — in a check as in a package pass
(0.5.0 allowed it only with a package request). Refused as today while the judge runs,
after it errored or expired, and in a handover pass (which has no judge).

**`_judgment_owed` (a check is `complete` only if every payment judge-due at its end was
covered).** It reads the judge row, which now always describes the latest judgment:
covered = due at the end in the same revision as in that judgment's `due_at_start`. The
latest judgment starts after the last chunk's filings, so it sees every document the pass
filed — at least what the single judge of 0.5.0 saw, which also started after the whole
Gmail round. Earlier judgments of the pass leave nothing to cover: a payment still due at
the end in an unchanged revision was due at the latest judgment's start too (its revision
and the fitting documents are what `judge_due_state` reads; the residual is the 0.3.1
one — a document's own edits during a judgment are that judgment's or the next pass's).
No change to `_judgment_owed`; it still never asks for a second judge step once one
exists ("end it interrupted").

### Skill changes (Ellen: the pass; shared rules)

- Step 3: the sweep delegation is `mode="async"`; at `pending` (always): record the
  delegation, say "Checking the bank — this takes a few minutes; I'll send the result
  here." (cron: `<silent/>`), end the turn.
- Step 4: the continuation's `work` is a chunk of at most 10 items, portals already left
  out; work it in parallel batches as in the package round.
- Step 5: after a chunk with items in it (the Gmail probe ok), always start the judge step
  and delegate it `mode="async"`; otherwise the existing conditions decide. At `pending`:
  record, `<silent/>`, end the turn.
- A judge continuation's `next` is `gmail-round` (the next chunk, then another judge) or
  `end-pass`, reporting the continuation's `report`.
- The handover flow's delegation stays `sync` (a single document; not in scope).

### Unchanged

`round_fate`, package rounds (except that `_hand_chunk` / `_another_chunk` now read the
pass's first step row — `sweep` or `snapshot` — instead of only `snapshot`), the claim
and token machinery, the delegation binding, `_round_judged`, handovers.

### Residuals (stated)

- As 0.5.0 for package rounds: a chunk turn that still dies (a chunk costing more than
  its budget, a Casa restart mid-turn), or a turn that dies between `delegate_to_agent`
  and `record_step(delegated)`, waits for the next `continue_pass`. Now a chunk of 10
  rather than a list of 50 opens that gap.
- Each chunk costs a judge delegation that re-triages every quarter and runs the sweep
  once more: more specialist time per check, bounded by each judge's 510 s wrap-up; no
  extra bank sync.
- "Go and check now" always answers "Checking the bank…" first; a small check no longer
  answers inside the operator's turn.

## Part B — issue #22: the date that names a file was read on the document

### What happens today (0.5.0)

Every machine pairing states `document_date`, read on the document (#19). Two kinds of
pairing never pass through that write: pairings made before 0.5.0 (a rebuild does not
fix them — the judge triages only payments without a pairing), and the operator's own
pairing of a document the specialist never judged. `package.doc_filename` names each
file `<document_date>_<issuer>_<amount>`, so these ship under the filed (often email)
date.

### The change

1. **The store records that a document's date was read.** Schema 7:
   `documents.date_read_at TEXT` (NULL: never read; existing rows NULL). Set to now, in
   the write's own transaction, by:
   - `record_match(author="auto")` and `propose_match` (they require `document_date`,
     read on the document — even when it equals the stored one);
   - `update_document_metadata` whenever `document_date` is among the fields (the
     specialist correcting or confirming it after reading the file; or Ellen writing a
     date the operator stated).
   Never by `ingest_document` (a provisional reading) or by an operator pairing.
2. **A package round's judge confirms the quarter's unread dates.**
   `list_quarter_state(quarter=<q>, dates_unread=true, pass_token)` lists, a page at a
   time in the listed shape, the quarter's payments whose current pairing (matched or
   proposed) holds a document with `date_read_at` NULL; each listed document shows
   `date_read`. In a package's judge step, after triage, the specialist reads each with
   `read_document` and calls `update_document_metadata(doc_id,
   document_date=<the printed issue date>, pass_token)` — the same value when it was
   right. What time does not reach, the next chunk's judge or the next package does.
3. **The package says what it could not confirm.** The build counts the files it names
   (matched and set-aside documents) whose date was never read. When any: the caption
   says "N files are named by a date not yet read from the document — listed in
   notes.md.", and notes.md gains a section listing those files. A package with none is
   byte-for-byte as before.

Not a gate: an unread date never holds a package back (the check's completeness is
unchanged). The operator owns what reaches the accountant; the plugin says what it
could not confirm.

### Residuals (stated)

- After the upgrade every filed document reads as unread, including those a 0.5.0
  machine pairing did read (0.5.0 kept no mark): the first package round of each quarter
  confirms them again. Until then the caption counts them.
- The value is the specialist's reading; the server cannot check it against the file.
- Only the current pairing's document is listed; a conflicted candidate set aside
  in `unresolved/` is counted by the caption but not listed for confirmation.

## Testing

- Part A: a check with 25 unsearched items runs sweep → chunk → judge → chunk → judge →
  chunk → judge → `end-pass` in one pass, each chunk ≤ 10, portals never in a chunk; the
  claim after each restarted judge is immediate; a chunk that searches nothing, an
  errored or expired judge, a failed Gmail probe each end the loop; `since_seq` survives
  a re-claimed sweep continuation; the report is cumulative (`checked` rises chunk by
  chunk, `not_searched` counts `not_fresh`); `_judgment_owed` accepts a check whose
  latest judgment covered what is due and refuses one where a payment reopened after it
  started; a judge restart in a handover pass, or while the judge runs, is refused;
  package rounds unchanged (their suite green).
- Part B: migration 6 → 7 on a frozen schema-6 store (DDL_V6 in `tests/schema_history.py`)
  leaves every document unread; `record_match(auto)`, `propose_match` and
  `update_document_metadata(document_date)` mark read, an operator pairing and an ingest
  do not; `list_quarter_state(dates_unread=true)` lists exactly the quarter's current
  pairings with unread dates; the caption and notes.md count them, and a package whose
  dates are all read has neither the caption line nor the notes.md section.
- Mutation-check each new guard (in a worktree).
