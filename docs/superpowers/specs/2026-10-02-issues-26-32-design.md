## The property this design keeps

From 0.5.0 on:

> **Every Ellen turn does a bounded amount of work, and ends at a delegation whose
> notification starts the next one.**

0.6.1 bounds the chunk turn by construction: capped costs (≤4 queries, ≤2 tries, one record
per item; ≤24 filing calls at the first chunk) against `ELLEN_TURNS` = 80. Part A3 keeps
that bound for the handed chunk and adds hand-outs **sized by the same caps from a count
Ellen reports**. That count is the one new trust assumption; Part A3 states what happens
when it is wrong.

## Part A — the open chunk (shared by #26, #28, #31)

A pass's Gmail round has, at any time, at most one **open chunk**: the pids handed out to
Ellen's current chunk turn. It is kept on the pass's first step row (`sweep` for a check,
`snapshot` for a package round, as `handed`/`owed`/`since_seq` are today), under the key
`chunk`:

```
chunk = {"pids": [...], "recorded": [...], "open": true, "calls": <int|null>, "more": <int>}
```

- **A1. Hand-out.** `_hand_chunk` (each continuation that says `next: "gmail-round"`) sets
  `chunk = {pids: <the handed items' pids>, recorded: [], open: true, calls: null, more: 0}`,
  replacing any earlier chunk. A re-claimed continuation (lease lapsed) hands out a new
  chunk, and the old one is gone with it. The old holder's token is refused anyway.
- **A2. Closing.** `record_step(step="judge", action="start")` sets `open: false`. A chunk
  is closed only by a judge start. A claim that hands out a new chunk replaces it, and a
  pass's end leaves it as it was.
- **A3. `more_work(pass_token, calls_made)` (new tool, #31).** Ellen calls it after every
  item of the open chunk is recorded. `calls_made` is her count of every tool call she has
  made in this turn so far, failed ones included, not counting this one. The server:
  1. checks the token. It refuses unless the chunk is open, this pass's latest Gmail probe
     is ok, and every chunk pid (merges resolved) is in `recorded`. The refusal names the
     unrecorded payments, or says to start the judge step.
  2. takes `used = max(calls_made, floor)`. The floor is the least the turn can have
     spent: at the first `more_work`, `3 + len(pids)` (continue_pass, the probe's search and
     record, one `record_search` per item); after that, the previous `used + 1 + (items it
     handed)`. A count below the floor is raised to the floor, never trusted.
  3. hands out `n = max(0, (ELLEN_TURNS - TURN_TAIL - 1 - MORE_MARGIN - used) // ITEM_COST)`
     more items. `MORE_MARGIN` = 8; `-1` is this call. The items are the next ones of
     the same work the chunk was cut from (Part A5 order), leaving out the chunk's pids. A
     check grows `owed` with them, as a hand-out does.
  4. appends them to `pids`, stores `calls = used`, `more = n`, and answers
     `{"triage": [work_item…], "next": "gmail-round" | "judge"}`. `next` is `judge` when
     `n` is 0 or the work is empty.

  `handed` (the count `_another_chunk` compares, `left < handed`) is not changed by
  `more_work`. It stays the work's size at the turn's hand-out, so the "fewer than when
  handed" progress test still compares the turn's start with its end.

  **The bound.** If `calls_made` is right, the turn ends within `used + 1 + n·ITEM_COST +
  TURN_TAIL ≤ ELLEN_TURNS - MORE_MARGIN`, by the same caps as the chunk. If Ellen
  undercounts by more than `MORE_MARGIN`, the turn can hit Casa's limit. Since
  ha-casa-app 0.332.22 (#1121), Casa then says so and asks the operator to say "continue".
  Every item recorded so far stays recorded, and the pass is claimable again after
  `LEASE_S`. That is a stall the operator sees, not a loss. The chunk itself (3/6) never
  depends on the count.

  Expected effect, from the measured ~4 calls per item: about 12 items per turn instead of 6
  with the same number of judge rounds.
- **A4. A search record belongs to the open chunk (#26, #28).** A `record_search` that
  carries effort (queries, `found_candidate` or `exhausted`) with a pass token is refused
  unless the pass has an open chunk and the payment (merge resolved) is one of its `pids`:
  "payment #N is not in the work you were handed: search and record only the chunk's
  items". Every accepted `record_search` on a chunk pid (effort, identity-only or bare
  `incomplete`) adds the pid to `recorded`. A call without effort is accepted anywhere,
  as today: the specialist's identity-only calls and the operator's quiet revive. This
  stops pass 3's pattern, where Ellen recorded items from her own list. It cannot stop a
  replayed record of a handed item (see #26).
- **A5. Order of hand-out (#31).** A chunk and `more_work` take items in the order
  `(tier is not required, searched_seq or 0, pid)`. Required items come first, then the
  never-searched, then the longest unsearched. A back-to-back check then starts where the
  previous one stopped, not at the lowest pid. Optional plan-fee rows (#31's
  observation) no longer come first. Paged listings (`list_quarter_state`) keep pid
  order. Only the hand-out is reordered.
- **A6. A check is not ended with its chunk unjudged (#28).** `end_pass` with `complete`
  or `interrupted` is refused while the pass's chunk is open with at least one pid and
  this pass's latest Gmail probe is not a failure (no probe yet counts as owed). The
  refusal says what is owed: "not ended: N payments handed out in this chunk are not
  searched and recorded yet — search each, record it, then start the judge step", or
  "…the chunk is recorded — start the judge step (record_step step=\"judge\") and end the
  pass after its continuation". It applies to check and package passes alike, and
  generalizes `_round_judged`, which only asked for some judge step. `stopped` and
  `failed` are not refused: they are the outcomes for a refusal or a failure, and the
  pass's report (#29) and review show what was left. A reclaimed pass
  (`_terminalize`) is not affected.

  Why this is safe: a refused `end_pass` leaves the pass live. If Ellen does nothing more,
  the next `continue_pass` (any notification, the operator's next accounting message, or
  the next cron's rule 1) claims it once the lease lapses and continues it.

## #26 — a replayed record can age a payment out

The server cannot see Gmail calls, and identical queries are not evidence of a replay (a
real re-run produces the same ladder). So the fix protects the asset, not the record:

- **B1. Age-out needs time between counted searches.** `passes_without_candidate` moves at
  most once per `AGE_OUT_SPACING_S` = 6 days (the weekly cron's cadence, with slack). It
  is kept as `search.last_counted_at`. A fruitless search within 6 days of the last
  counted one is recorded (queries, `searched_seq`, report) but does not move the count. So
  back-to-back checks, honest or replayed, cannot age a payment out. Three fruitless
  searches spread over at least 12 days are needed. Migration: a record without
  `last_counted_at` but with `last_counted_pass` takes its `last_searched_at` as
  `last_counted_at`. The conservative reading: the last record may have counted.
- **B2.** A4: only handed items can be recorded with effort.
- **B3. Skill.** "Record only the queries you ran with `search_emails` in this turn, for
  this item. A query from an earlier turn or pass, or one you planned but did not run, is
  never recorded. An item you did not search is recorded with no queries and
  `incomplete=true`."

Residual, stated: a replayed record of a handed item still counts as "checked" in the
check's report. Nothing the server sees can tell it from a real search.

## #27 — a payee-unknown item comes back in every chunk

- **C1.** An identity-only `record_search` (`identity_unknown=true`, no effort) also stamps
  `search.identity_seq = next_seq` and `search.identity_fp` (the facts, as `facts_fp`).
- **C2.** `check_work` and `package_work` leave out an item whose identity was recorded
  since the work's origin (the check's `since_seq`, the request's `created_seq`) for the
  same facts. So it is not handed out again in this check or round. The next check
  (a new origin) hands it out again. The age-out count is untouched, as today.
- **C3.** `check_report` counts such an item as checked: the check did what it can for it,
  which is to ask who the payee is. So the report's `not_searched` and the outcome rule
  ("interrupted when an item is not searched") no longer leave every check with an unknown
  payee `interrupted`. `package_check.unsearched` follows `package_work`.

## #29 — the stored report is the server's

- **E1.** `end_pass` strips `checked`, `total` and `not_searched` from the caller's
  `report`. For a pass with a check origin (`since_seq` on its first step), it stores
  `_check_report` computed in its own transaction. Otherwise it stores no counts.
  `_terminalize` (a reclaimed pass) stores the same server counts. The `end_pass` answer
  carries the stored `report`.
- **E2.** The review's "Review interrupted" block prints "N of M new payments checked" only
  when the stored report has the counts. Otherwise it prints "Review interrupted." and
  "Saved.".

## #30 — an invoice in another currency is never paired

The server accepts a pairing in any currency; the gap is the skill's bar.

- **D1. Skill, the auto-match bar.** "The gross amount and currency are exactly equal — or
  the document is in another currency and prints the payment's exact amount in the
  payment's currency (the amount charged, or a total at a printed rate that gives exactly
  that amount)."
- **D2. Skill, a proposal.** A document in another currency that is the vendor's, of the
  expected kind, and dated within the vendor's window, with no printed amount in the
  payment's currency: `propose_match` with a rationale naming both amounts. If several
  fit, the closest date wins and the others are `runners_up` with `guessed`. A proposal
  shows in "anything I should check?" and ships `UNCONFIRMED` with its file, never as
  `MISSING`.
- **D3. Views.** A pairing or candidate whose document's currency differs from the
  payment's gets the line "The invoice is in USD 11.00; the bank charged EUR 9.48."
  (kind word from `KIND_WORD`). In the package, the row's notes say
  "document in USD 11.00".
- **D4. Upstream.** A casa-specialist-finance issue asks bank-feed to expose a row's
  exchange rate (`get_transaction`, `export_history`). With it, a later release can
  auto-pair within ±0.02 of EUR × rate.

## #32 — "0.00" in a package file name

- **F1.** `doc_filename` names the amount only when one is stored:
  `<date>_<issuer>.<ext>` otherwise. A stored 0 is still "0.00".
- **F2.** A machine pairing (`record_match` auto, `propose_match`) is refused for a document
  with no stored amount: "read the document's amount and currency and
  update_document_metadata first". The auto-match bar compares amounts, so a pairing
  without one was never shown to meet it.
