## r1 folds (Astra DO NOT SHIP, Terra DO NOT SHIP; same shape: the purchase missed a link)
Generalised, not extended. A purchase is the CONNECTED COMPONENT of two links, closed in
`documents.purchase` (breadth-first): the same issuer and number; or a shared email
message with the same read amount and currency, where a document's messages are EVERY
email ref it holds (its `source_ref` and the `operator_refs` rows of the same bytes filed
again). Every write that can grow a held document's purchase is checked against the job's
floor (`_one_purchase_one_payment`, job token only), with the purchase captured before
ANY of the write: `update_document_metadata` (an amount completed later), and a duplicate
filing (`ingest_document`'s same-bytes path). The Review legend counts the walk's own unit,
vendors ("the missing invoices of 6 vendors"): a vendor's card walks that vendor's missing
payments of every quarter. The completion pins in test_issue_47 are re-aimed at the posted
card.

## r2 folds (Astra DO NOT SHIP, Terra DO NOT SHIP)
- **The floor is ONE invariant** (an unheld bridge or a new filing could join two held
  purchases). After every job write that can change a purchase — a new filing, the same
  bytes filed again, a reading (`update_document_metadata`) — the payments backing the
  written document's purchase must all have backed ONE purchase that existed before the
  write. The purchases before are: the document's own (captured before the write; `[doc]`
  for a new filing), and, for each document the write joined, its purchase taken without
  the written document. That is exact, since the write changed only that document's links.
  Otherwise the write is refused and rolls back. A 0.11.0 purchase already backing two
  payments takes a write that joins nothing new to it. The operator's filings and words
  (no token) are not limited.
- *(Superseded by r3.)* **The email link is an invoice and its own receipt:** same email,
  same read amount and currency, the same issuer, and ANOTHER kind (r2 Terra S1: two different invoices in one
  digest email with equal amounts merged and refused the second payment's own document).
  A refiled copy of the same invoice still links by its number. Residual: one email holding
  an invoice and a receipt of two DIFFERENT purchases of the same vendor and amount merges
  them.
- **Every end-card branch sets the package flag** (an empty quarter offered [Get package]).
- **test_issue_47's freeze pin** runs a completion without a delivered card, so the frozen
  line is a real line.

## r3 folds (Astra DO NOT SHIP, Terra SHIP WITH FIXES) — ESCALATION
The email link drew a too-wide finding a third time (r2: two digest invoices; r3: a statement
bridging two invoices). Escalated: the link is replaced by the measured fact and nothing
wider. **In each email a document was filed from, the documents with its issuer and read
amount and currency must be EXACTLY one invoice and one receipt, it one of them; then the
other is its email twin.** A statement, a second invoice, or two pairs in that email make no
email link there (they fall back to issuer + number). This supersedes r2's "another kind".
It is a new mechanism and gets its own focused round (r4). The measured casa-test shape (21
emails, one invoice plus its receipt each) links in full.
- The end card's replace questions are its quarter's on every branch (Astra S2).
- A multi-pick legend names every pick button: "INV-88 (2 Aug), INV-91 (4 Aug): use this
  document" (Terra S2).

## §1 #53: the render tag becomes a time with seconds

- `views.tag_for(render_id)` → `tag_at(moment)`: `" · HH:MM:SS"`. It is the composition time
  (`db._clock()`) in the operator's zone: `CASA_TZ`, else `TZ`, else UTC. That is Casa's
  `timekeeping.resolve_tz` order; an unknown zone name falls back to UTC.
- Every call site that ends line 1 with a tag uses the same function:
  - `views._review` (views);
  - `cards._store` (cards);
  - `posting._deposit_package` (the file caption).
- `cards.TAG_WORST` becomes `" · 00:00:00"`, a fixed width.
- **Binding is unchanged.** `bound_rendering` already compares the whole normalised
  quote; the tag is just text inside it.
- **What changes:** two renderings with identical text composed in the same second share
  their tag.
  - With the same binding facts, the lowest render id binds (V3: harmless).
  - With differing facts (e.g. a paged view's `next.prev`), the reply gets AMBIGUOUS, a
    visible refusal with 0 steps. The recovery view is composed later, so it carries a
    later second and binds.
  - This residual was accepted by the operator.
- **Never:** the tag is never parsed back into a render id. A grep shows nothing reads it.

## §2 #55: two intents, one tool path each

The desk skill describes the two intents in prose. It has no verb or phrase lists, and
its examples are marked as illustrations.

**Where a quarter stands: `show_view(view="open", quarter=Q)`.** Nothing runs.
- **Q inside the books:** `cards.compose_open(conn, Q)` becomes a **quarter status card**.
  - Head: `Q3 · {n} payments` (or `Q3 · {n} payments · all accounted for`).
  - Counts line: §4 (non-zero only).
  - "To confirm:" lists Q's proposals only. Vendor walk items: Q's missing only.
  - One line per OTHER quarter with open items: `Q4 so far: 1 to confirm · 2 missing`
    (non-zero parts only).
  - Buttons: ⟦Review (N)⟧ ⟦Confirm all (K)⟧ ⟦Get package⟧, then the §5 legend line. The
    package button appears only when Q has at least one in-scope payment.
  - Today the open-items card is store-wide. Restricting it to Q is the #55 complaint ("a
    Q4 proposal inside a Q3 answer").
  - The Recovery use (`view="open"` with no quarter, §1 of the simple loop) keeps
    `main_quarter`.
- **Q before the books' start** (`Q < quarter_of(watermark)`): no rendering, nothing
  deposited. `show_view` returns `{"say": "Q3 2026 isn't in the books yet: they start 1
  Oct 2026. Ask me to do Q3 and I'll start the books from 1 Jul."}`, and the skill says it
  verbatim.

**Get Q done: `request_work(kind="check", trigger="operator", quarter=Q)`, then `start_job`**
(the existing ask path).
- **Q inside the books:** unchanged (line `Checking the bank and your email — …`).
- **Q before the books' start** (operator ruling 2026-10-08, "just do it, no question"):
  in the SAME transaction that queues the ask, the watermark moves to Q's first day
  (`work.set_watermark_in_tx`'s body, through a new internal `work.start_from_in_tx(conn,
  Q)` that takes no grant). It is reachable only from `request_work` with
  `trigger="operator"` and a named quarter.
  - The line: `Starting the books from 1 Jul 2026 and checking Q3 — I'll post the result
    here.`
  - A cron ask never moves the start.
  - Moving it later stays refused (`day >= watermark` → nothing moves; such a Q is not
    "before" anyway).
- **Removed:** the reading verb `start` (reply.py `("start", …)`, its PHRASE row, its
  `setting` branch).
  - A request that implies an earlier start is the "get Q done" intent for that quarter.
  - `cards.nothing_to_check` and the views' watermark announcement stop quoting
    `'start from …'`. They say `Ask me to do Q2 …` instead (an illustration, not a
    trigger).
  - `work.set_watermark_in_tx` keeps its grant check. It has no caller after the removal,
    so it is deleted rather than left dead (`check_removed.py` list updated).

**The end of a run (2a/2b):**
- The end card (`compose_end`) keeps its role as the one card.
- Its head for the run's quarter Q is `Q3 checked · {n} payments`. Its proposals and
  missing walk are the store's open items, as today: a run works every in-scope payment.
  Other quarters are summarised as today's `earlier` lines, now with non-zero parts only.
- A run never starts for a quarter before the books: (2b) moves the start first.
- **The duplicate end text:**
  - Casa hands the job's completion text (`emit_completion`) to the resident as a
    DelegationComplete. The resident's turn is what relayed "Nothing to check for Q3…" on
    prod (#1301 silences only QUIET scheduled runs).
  - The plugin's lever is the text. `job.run_end` on a completed run whose end card was
    composed returns `The result card is posted in the chat; there is nothing to add.` in
    place of `checked_line`.
  - `checked_line` stays for a run that composed no card.
  - Full suppression would need a Casa change; out of lane, and noted for BRAIN.

## §3 #52: one purchase is one line and one button everywhere

- `cards._purchases` (done) counts the proposal line.
- **The Review card** (`_proposal_card`) groups `_offered` by purchase key (the same
  `db.purchase_key(issuer)` + `db.purchase_key(number)`; a numberless document is its own
  group).
  - Each group shows ONE line and at most one pick button, for its representative: the
    current document if the group holds it, else the group's `invoice`-kind document,
    else its first.
  - The other members are not displayed and not bound: `docs` and `picks` list only
    representatives, so a tap can only name a displayed document (binding unchanged).
  - `picks` exist when there are ≥ 2 groups, or when there is no current document (as
    today, counted in groups).
- The mirror note's "(or N other invoices)" is not changed (the bank note; not in the
  script).

## §4 Zero counts are not shown

- `_counts_line(c)` prints only non-zero buckets (order: matched, need no invoice, to
  confirm, missing, pending).
- An all-zero Counter cannot reach it: `n == 0` takes the `nothing_to_check` branch.
- The other-quarter lines likewise.
- The scheduled head `Q3 · new: {a} to confirm · {b} missing` likewise.

## §5 Plain words

**Receipts and reading phrases** (reply.PHRASE, taps receipts):

| Today | New |
|---|---|
| Unpair {h} / Unpaired {h} | Remove the match for {h} / Removed the match for {h} |
| Set aside {n} for {h} | Rule out {n} for {h} / Ruled out {n} for {h} |
| Paired {h} (a pick) | Matched {h} |
| Unpaired {h} and set aside its N other candidates | Removed the match for {h} and ruled out its N other invoices |
| Set aside both candidates for {h} | Ruled out both invoices for {h} |

- `views.evidence`'s "Paired with {doc}." becomes "Matched to {doc}." Likewise
  "…after it was paired".
- cards `_handover`'s "Filed. Paired with …" becomes "Filed. Matched to …".
- `views.FORBIDDEN` gains `pair` (word-boundary check in the pin test, so "repair" is
  not affected) and `set aside`.

**Buttons** (operator correction 2026-10-08): short labels stay, counts go in parentheses
(⟦Review (2)⟧, ⟦Confirm all (1)⟧, ⟦Get package⟧); the vendor, replace and review-card
labels are unchanged except that a pick reads `<number> (1 Sep)`. A **legend line** ends
every CARD that carries buttons (cards.KINDS, the reading proposal, the account choice):
one line, `Label: what it does` for each button actually shown, joined by " · ", from one
table `cards.LEGEND` (Review: see each open item and decide · Confirm all: accept the
proposed invoices · Get package: the Q3 zip for your accountant · a pick: use this
document · Confirm: this invoice is right · Wrong: not this one, keep looking · Leave for
now: decide later · No invoice needed: these need none · Never for X: X never sends one ·
Leave missing: stop looking, keep them missing · Next page: the rest of this vendor · Keep
current / Use new: keep the filed invoice / use the new one · Apply: make this change ·
Cancel: change nothing · Account n: this is the business account). Picks share ONE entry
("<number>: use this document"). The legend is computed from the same function that makes
the buttons (`cards.buttons` is refactored to read the stored scope and kind, so `_store`
can compute the labels before inserting) and it is part of the fitted text: `_fits` and
`_fit_count` measure it like any line, it is never bound, and it sits after the last
bound line, so a fit that must cut cuts it first (Undisplayed is still raised only for a
bound line). **Views** (status/missing/item pages: All good, One by one, More, …) are not
changed in this release: their budgets are computed before the buttons, and the script's
flows no longer post them; flagged for BRAIN.
